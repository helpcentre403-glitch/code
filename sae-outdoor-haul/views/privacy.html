Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how {{BUSINESS_NAME}} collects and uses your information when you use this website or receive text messages from us.

Who we are

{{BUSINESS_NAME}} ("we," "us") provides junk removal, landscaping, and Christmas tree delivery and installation in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. Contact: {{PHONE_DISPLAY}}, {{EMAIL_HTML}}.

What we collect

When you request an estimate or booking, we collect your name, mobile phone number, email (optional), service address, the date and time you pick, your answers about the job, and whether you agreed to receive text messages from us (with the date and time you agreed).

How we use it

  • To calculate your estimate, including measuring driving distance to your address.
  • To schedule and perform the work.
  • To contact you about your estimate and booking by phone, email, or text message (texts only if you agreed to receive them).

Text messages (SMS)

If you check the box to receive texts when booking, {{BUSINESS_NAME}} will send text messages to the mobile number you provide, such as your booking confirmation, a receipt of your estimate, and updates about your appointment. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your mobile number and text-message consent are used only by {{BUSINESS_NAME}} to send you the messages described above.

Service providers

We use service providers only to run this website and our services, and they may receive only the information needed for their task: Render (website hosting), Twilio (sending text messages), Google (email), OpenStreetMap-based services or Google Maps Platform (measuring driving distance and suggesting addresses), and Anthropic (an optional AI service that reviews your answers and writes the estimate summary). We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and analytics

If turned on, this site uses the Meta Pixel to measure our Facebook and Instagram ads. It tells Meta when a page is viewed, an estimate is shown, a time is booked, or a call or text link is tapped, along with information your browser sends, such as cookies. We do not send Meta your name, phone number, email, or address. See Meta's Privacy Policy for how Meta uses this information.

How long we keep it

We keep estimate and booking records for as long as we need them to serve you and keep our business records. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information by calling or texting {{PHONE_DISPLAY}} or emailing {{EMAIL_HTML}}.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
